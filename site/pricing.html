Questions

About it being free

Why is it free?

Because the point is for people to have it. A macro pad shouldn't cost $150 when a $10 numpad can do the job.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. NumpadControl runs entirely on your computer, so there are no servers to pay for, and it never shows ads or collects data.

Will it stay free?

Yes, that's the whole idea. And it's all open source, so it can't be taken away from anyone.

Do I need to buy anything?

Just a USB numpad, wired or wireless. Cheap ones work fine.
