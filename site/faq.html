FAQ

Questions, answered

Something missing? Ask on GitHub.

Your numpad

Which numpads work?

Any USB numpad, wired or with a wireless receiver. The ones people have tested are listed on GitHub, and you can add yours with a two-minute guided test.

My keyboard already has a number pad. Can I use that?

NumpadPilot is made for a separate numpad, so your main keyboard (number pad included) keeps typing normally. An old spare keyboard works as the "numpad" too.

What about the odd "000" key?

It becomes a key of its own. Saving the OBS replay buffer is a favourite.

What happens if I unplug it?

Nothing breaks: you get a notification, and it's picked up again as soon as you plug it back in.

Games

Can it get me banned?

NumpadPilot installs no driver and nothing in the system's core, so there's nothing for anti-cheat to flag. On top of that, while a game with strict anti-cheat runs (Valorant, League of Legends, Fortnite, Call of Duty, Apex Legends, Battlefield and any game using Easy Anti-Cheat or BattlEye), it lets go of your numpad completely and takes it back when the game closes.

Does it work in games at all, then?

In most games, yes. In the protected ones above, the numpad types normally while the game runs: that's the safe choice. You can add your own games to the list.

Privacy

It reads my keys. Does it record what I type?

No. It only acts on your numpad's keys, everything stays on your computer, and it collects nothing: no telemetry, no statistics, no account. NumpadPilot is open source, so anyone can check.

What if it crashes?

A plain dialog asks whether to report it. If you say yes, your browser opens the report so you can read every word first; if you say no, nothing is sent.

Computers

Windows?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. No driver, no administrator rights. A few apps running as administrator don't let it hide the numpad's keys from them; its actions still work there.

Linux?

Yes, first-class: made on Linux, tested on KDE Plasma and Hyprland. Most actions work on any desktop.

Mac?

Not yet: Windows and Linux first. Join the waitlist and pick "Something else" to tell us you're on a Mac.
