Ready in two minutes

No scripts, no config files, no second keyboard getting in the way.

Step 1

Plug in any numpad

A $10 wired one, a wireless one, the one in your drawer. NumpadPilot tells it apart from your main keyboard, even if that keyboard has its own number pad.

Step 2

Press a key on it

NumpadPilot finds your numpad by itself. From now on, only its keys run actions; your main keyboard types as usual.

Step 3

Pick what each key does

Press, hold or double-tap: three actions per key. Add a layer key and every key gets a second set.

Questions

Which numpads work?

Any USB numpad, wired or with a wireless receiver. The ones we've tested are listed on GitHub, and you can add yours with a two-minute test.

Will it get me banned in games?

NumpadPilot installs no driver, so there's nothing in your system for anti-cheat to flag. On top of that, it lets go of your numpad completely while games with strict anti-cheat (Valorant, Fortnite, Call of Duty and others) are running, and takes it back when they close.

Does it record what I type?

No. It only acts on your numpad's keys, everything stays on your computer, and it collects nothing. The engine is open source, so anyone can check.
