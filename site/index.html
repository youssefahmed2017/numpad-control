How it works

Ready in two minutes

No scripts, no config files, no second keyboard getting in the way.

1
Plug in any numpad

A $10 wired one, a wireless one, the one in your drawer. NumpadControl tells it apart from your main keyboard, even if that keyboard has its own number pad.

2
Press a key on it

NumpadControl finds your numpad by itself. From then on its keys only run actions, and your main keyboard types as usual.

3
Pick what each key does

Press, hold or double-tap: three actions per key. Hold the layer key and every key gets a second set.
